# Три отметки — original artwork

Copyright (c) 2026 Три отметки (triotmetki.ru). All rights reserved.

Covers the crosshair marks (`otmetki/crosshair`), the sixth-sense icons (`otmetki/sixth_sense`) and the
damage-log glyphs (`otmetki/damage_log`): the SVG sources and the PNG renditions made from them; and the
colour grading of the hangar looks (`otmetki/hangar_looks`): the grades and the colour tables written from them.

- Drawn from scratch for the «Три отметки» modpack in its own style (graphite, orange and gold of
  `@otmetki/design-tokens`). Popular packs (Джов, Near_You, Kotyarko, ПРОТанки, XVM skins) were looked at
  as a visual reference only — layout, sizes, readability, colour conventions. No artwork of theirs was
  traced, copied, recoloured or derived from.
- Distributed only as part of the modpack (free and subscription editions). Not for redistribution on its
  own without written permission.
